# V5 design notes

The V5 workflow takes useful ideas from modern scan-first companion apps while keeping an original HeroLens layout and feature set.

## Included product ideas

- Dedicated scan screen with a large camera viewport
- Quick zoom and tap-to-focus
- Rank selection
- History and settings tabs
- Best pick plus alternatives
- Scan confidence and hero portraits
- Clear counter/synergy explanations

## Additional HeroLens enhancements

- Personal hero-pool weighting
- Ultimate-charge switch warning
- Multi-frame consensus instead of accepting one frame
- Blur, darkness, and glare rejection
- Optional detection boxes
- Manual correction for uncertain slots
- Composition-coverage comparison instead of presenting a fake predicted win probability
- Local-only history and privacy-first defaults
- No premium lock on counter/synergy explanations in the prototype
