HeroLens prototype privacy

The browser prototype processes live camera frames in browser memory for hero recognition. It does not upload camera frames, scoreboard images, or recommendation history to a server.

Recent scan history and preferences are stored locally in the browser. Clearing site data removes them.

Hero portrait images are requested from their configured image host. The native Android MVP caches portrait templates locally for recognition.

This is an unofficial technical prototype and not affiliated with Blizzard Entertainment.
